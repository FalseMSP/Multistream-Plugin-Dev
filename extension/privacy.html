RedStreamIntegration — Privacy Notice

Privacy notice for the RedStreamIntegration Twitch Extension on YOUR-CHANNEL-NAME's channel.

What this extension is

RedStreamIntegration is a viewer panel that lets viewers submit requests to the streamer's queue — for example a game level to play, with an optional note. Requests submitted through the panel join the same queue as the streamer's chat commands, and the queue (including who submitted each request) is displayed on the channel page while it is active.

Data the extension collects

Username

Read automatically from your signed-in Twitch session so your request can be credited to you. You never type it, and it cannot be faked by someone else. It is shown next to your request in the public queue.

Viewer ID

An identifier Twitch assigns to you as a viewer of the panel. If you are logged out of Twitch, it is used instead of a username and appears as a stable “Viewer-XXXX” handle.

Any other details submitted through the extension

Whatever you choose to submit with a request — for example a level ID, or an optional note of up to 80 characters. These details are shown with your request in the queue. Submitting them is entirely up to you.

What the extension does NOT collect

  • No email address, payment info, or account details
  • No advertising or tracking of any kind
  • No analytics or third-party scripts
  • No cookies — nothing is stored in your browser
  • No data about your activity anywhere outside this panel

Where data lives and who can see it

Submitted requests are stored only on the server the streamer runs to operate the queue (their own bot). The queue — your username and the details you submitted — is visible by design to anyone viewing the channel page while the queue is active, and to the streamer. Your username is also processed by Twitch itself when it signs you in; that part is governed by Twitch's own privacy policy.

How long data is kept

Data submitted through the extension is embedded in video files, as VODs will last on YouTube and Twitch — refer to Twitch's and YouTube's policies for further information.
